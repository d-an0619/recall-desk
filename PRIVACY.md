# Recall Desk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Recall Desk is a Forge app for Jira Service Management. It runs entirely on Atlassian Forge.

## What the app reads
- The summary and description of the Jira issue you open, only to find a vehicle identification number (VIN).
- The VIN you type into the panel or give to the Recall Desk Rovo agent.

## What the app sends outside Atlassian
- The VIN, and the make, model and model year decoded from it, are sent to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) public APIs (`vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov`, `api.nhtsa.gov`) to look up vehicle details and safety recalls. No other data is sent.

## What the app stores
- Nothing. The app does not store personal data, issue content, VINs or lookup results. It keeps no logs of its own beyond Atlassian's standard Forge platform logs.
